Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic italian comfort_food dish made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of meat, vegetables, sauces and cheese then baking in oven.

  • Flat pasta
  • Sauces
  • cheese
  • Fillings(liking of meat and vegetables)
  1. Prepare the sauces(meat sauce,cheese sauce or any you like)
  2. Layer the dish(in baking pan spread thin layer of sauce then pasta,meat,cheese,vegetables,sauce,pasta then repeat.)
  3. Bake in oven for 30 to 45 mins (180c-190c or 350f-375f)
  4. Rest for 10 to 15 mins before serving.
