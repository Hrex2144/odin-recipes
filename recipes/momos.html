Momos

Description

Make classic street-style vegetable momos at home using simple ingredients and a quick steaming method.

  • all-purpose flour
  • soy sauce
  • cheese
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Fillings( 2 cups finely chopped cabbage , 1 grated carrot , 1 finely chopped onion or spring onion (with greens) , 1 tsp finely chopped ginger , 1 tsp finely chopped garlic , 1-2 green chilies, finely chopped . 1 tsp soy sauce ,½ tsp black pepper powder ,1 tsp oil (for sautéing))
  1. Combine the all-purpose flour and salt in a mixing bowl. Add 1 tablespoon of oil. Gradually pour in warm water and knead into a smooth, semi-soft dough. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for 30 minute
  2. Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a pan on medium-high heat. Add the minced ginger, garlic, and green chilies; sauté for 30 seconds. Toss in the chopped onions, cabbage, and grated carrots. Sauté on high heat for 2 to 3 minutes so the vegetables soften slightly without releasing too much water. Mix in soy sauce, black pepper powder, and salt. Turn off the heat and let the filling cool down completely
  3. • Divide the rested dough into small, equal-sized balls. • Roll out each ball into a very thin, small circle (around 3 inches wide), keeping the edges slightly thinner than the center. • Place 1 tablespoon of the vegetable filling in the center of the wrapper. • Pleat the edges together and twist at the top to seal into a round or crescent sha
  4. • Lightly grease a steamer plate or tray with oil so the momos do not stick. • Place the momos on the tray, leaving a little space between each one. • Steam over boiling water on medium-high heat for 10 to 12 minutes until the outer dough turns glossy and translucent
