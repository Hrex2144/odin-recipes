Pan cake

Description

Pan cake You can make classic, fluffy homemade pancakes quickly with a few simple pantry staple.

  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 ¼ cups milk
  • 3 tablespoons butter, melted (plus extra for the pan)
  • 1 large eg
  1. Mix dry ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt
  2. Combine wet and dry: Make a well in the center of the dry mixture. Pour in the milk, melted butter, and egg. Mix just until smooth—do not overmix.
  3. Bake in oven for 30 to 45 mins (180c-190c or 350f-375f)
  4. Heat the pan: Lightly oil or butter a griddle or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. 1. Cook the pancakes: Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake onto the griddle. Cook until bubbles form on top and the edges look dry, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. Flip and finish: Flip carefully and cook until browned on the other side. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.
